Participant Information Sheet — AwareML Main Study

Study: AwareML Trust Calibration and Human Oversight Study
Institution: University of Tartu, Institute of Computer Science
Research contact: Vikash Maheshwari — maheshwari@ut.ee

Purpose

This study examines whether users calibrate trust in AI-generated explanations appropriately and whether they seek additional evidence before relying on an AutoML recommendation.

What you will do

You will evaluate 20 AI explanation items. Each item includes a task, an AI explanation, and reference evidence. You will rate trust, perceived factual correctness, fluency, and confident-sounding language, then decide whether you would Accept, Override, or Reject the explanation. You may also complete a short questionnaire about human control, explanation usefulness, and usability.

A smaller optional Information-Seeking activity may ask you to inspect more evidence or ask follow-up questions before making a final decision.

Eligibility and voluntariness

Participants must be at least 18 years old. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty and without giving a reason. People who helped author the Track-1 scenario/stimulus bank should not take part in this study.

Data collected

AwareML stores a pseudonymous participant/session identifier, expertise group and self-rating, study responses, response times, Accept/Override/Reject decisions, questionnaire answers, and—if you take part in Information Seeking—follow-up categories, evidence views, timing, final decision, and task rationale. The study is designed not to place names or raw dataset rows in the study response store.

Risks and benefits

This is assessed by the research team as a minimal-risk behavioral study. Possible burdens include mild fatigue or uncertainty when judging deliberately imperfect explanations. There is no guaranteed direct personal benefit, but the study may contribute to safer and more human-centred AutoML systems.

Confidentiality

Responses are stored under pseudonymous identifiers and are accessible only to the research team. Participant-facing records are kept separate from researcher-only ground-truth labels. Main Study response data must not be sent as raw participant data to external generative-AI services. Results will be reported in aggregate form; any illustrative quotations will be de-identified.

Retention and withdrawal

Research data are planned to be retained for five years after publication of the associated AwareML journal article and then deleted or irreversibly anonymized, subject to University of Tartu record-management requirements. You may request withdrawal of linkable pseudonymous study data by providing your participant code before the data are irreversibly anonymized or aggregated.
