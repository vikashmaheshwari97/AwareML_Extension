AwareML Human-Study Ethics Self-Assessment and Submission Summary

Study

AwareML Extension: Trust Calibration, Human Agency, and Information-Seeking in Streaming AutoML

Institution: University of Tartu, Institute of Computer Science
Research contact: Vikash Maheshwari — maheshwari@ut.ee

Research-team risk classification

Minimal-risk behavioral/social-science research.

This is a research-team self-assessment for ethics submission. It is not an ethics approval, exemption, or not-required determination.

Participants

Adults aged 18 or older. The study recruits novice/student, practitioner, and ML/AutoML-expert participants. Track-2 participants must not have authored the Track-1 scenario/stimulus bank.

Phase 16 — Trust Calibration

Within-subject, randomized and blinded design. Each participant evaluates 20 explanation items: 10 known-correct and 10 known-incorrect variants drawn from matched pairs. Ground-truth correctness is hidden during participation.

Primary question: whether trust is higher for correct than incorrect explanations rather than being driven mainly by fluency/confident-sounding language.

Primary outcome: within-participant correct-minus-incorrect trust difference using the finalized validated trust measure.

Secondary outcomes: perceived factual correctness, fluency, confident-sounding language, Accept/Override/Reject behavior, response time, appropriate reliance, over-trust, under-trust, expertise-stratified descriptive results, and post-study human-agency/study-experience measures.

Recruitment target: up to 40; planned minimum complete sample: 34.

Phase 17 — Information Seeking

Optional qualitative/behavioral sub-study conducted after Trust Calibration. Target 12 participants, preferably approximately 4 novice, 4 practitioner and 4 expert participants.

The activity records whether users ask for further evidence, follow-up depth/type, time to first follow-up, evidence areas viewed, final recommendation decision, confidence and task rationale. Automated classification is assistive only; manual qualitative coding remains authoritative.

Risks

Expected risks are no greater than ordinary computer-based questionnaire/usability research: mild fatigue, temporary uncertainty or frustration while comparing deliberately imperfect explanations, and possible discomfort at discovering that some explanations were intentionally incorrect.

Mitigations: participants may stop at any time; the study does not evaluate participant competence; a debrief explains the correct/incorrect manipulation; no sensitive personal information is required; free-text instructions prohibit entering personal or sensitive data.

Data minimization

Collected study data: pseudonymous participant/session identifier, expertise group/self-rating, trial responses and response times, reliance decisions, secondary questionnaire responses, and Information-Seeking event/timing/category/evidence-view records.

Not intentionally collected: names in the study response store, raw dataset rows, health data, political/religious data, biometric data, passwords or credentials.

Confidentiality and security

Participant-facing records and researcher-only ground truth are separated. Main Study response data must not be sent to external generative-AI services as raw participant-response data. Researcher access uses a private deployment key. Final collection uses a private participant-ID salt and a separate Main Study store.

Consent and withdrawal

Consent is active opt-in. Participation consent and consent to process study responses are requested separately. Participants confirm age 18+. Withdrawal is allowed without penalty. A participant code is used to locate pseudonymous records for withdrawal before irreversible anonymization/aggregation.

Retention

Research data are planned for retention for five years after publication of the associated AwareML journal article, then deletion or irreversible anonymization, subject to University of Tartu record-management requirements.

Determination requested

Please determine whether this study requires formal approval, qualifies for exemption, or is otherwise not required to undergo committee review under the applicable University of Tartu procedure. The Main Study will not begin until a written institutional determination is recorded.
