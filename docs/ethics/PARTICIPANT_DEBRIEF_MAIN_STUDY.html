Main Study Debrief

Thank you for participating.

The study deliberately contained both correct and incorrect explanation variants while hiding the condition during evaluation. This was necessary to test whether trust and reliance track explanation correctness rather than fluency or confident-sounding presentation.

The study was not testing your intelligence or technical competence. Accepting an explanation was not automatically considered good or bad: appropriate reliance depends on whether the explanation was supported by the reference evidence.

Your responses are stored under a pseudonymous study identifier and will be analysed according to the frozen study protocol.
