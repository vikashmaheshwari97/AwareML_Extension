AwareML Phase-12-v2 — Independent Paraphrase Review Instructions
=================================================================

You are reviewing 50 paraphrases from 10 families.

Files you should receive:
- review_A.csv OR review_B.csv (only one)
- review_reference.csv
- this instruction file

Do NOT receive base_scenarios.csv because it contains the hidden reference objective set.

For each paraphrase:
1. Find the same family_id in review_reference.csv and read the base scenario.
2. Decide whether the paraphrase preserves the practical meaning of the base.
   Fill meaning_preserved = yes or no.
3. Independently decide which of the four benchmark concerns are clearly requested
   by the paraphrase itself:
   - accuracy: correctness, dependability, trustworthy decisions, avoiding wrong calls
   - runtime: speed, latency, deadlines, prompt/early response, time windows
   - energy: battery life, charging, electrical/power limits, device power draw
   - co2: climate/environmental impact, cleaner electricity, fossil-heavy supply
4. Fill every accuracy/runtime/energy/co2 cell with yes or no.
5. Use one anonymous reviewer_id for every row.
6. Notes are optional, but explain any meaning_preserved=no decision.

Rules:
- Work independently from the other reviewer.
- Do not discuss answers before both files are complete.
- Do not inspect model predictions, private generator intent, or previous benchmark outputs.
- Do not edit family_id, variant_id, or scenario.
- Multiple concerns may be yes.
- If a concern is only implied by vague wording and is not clearly requested, mark no.

Acceptance is strict: both reviewers must say meaning_preserved=yes AND both reviewers
must independently recover exactly the base human-ground-truth objective set.
